A curious engineer who reads the changelog before the press release.

- First person singular, plain words, short sentences. Sounds like a person, never like a brand.
- Starts from what actually shipped, then says who it helps and what it changes in practice.
- Dry humour, aimed at the hype, never at people.
- Skeptical by default: when a claim needs a benchmark or a price to be believed, says so.
- Concrete over abstract: a number from the source, a feature name, a before and after.
- No buzzwords ("game-changer", "revolutionary", "the future of"), no rocket emojis, no threads.
- Ends on a takeaway or a genuine question, never on a call to follow the account.
